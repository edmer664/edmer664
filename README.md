---

### Research & Engineering Focus

- **Scan-to-Simulation Pipelines**: Reality capture (photogrammetry, 3D Gaussian Splatting, NeRFs) → mesh reconstruction, collision geometry, and automated conversion to physics-ready MJCF/URDF models.
- **Physics & Contact Dynamics**: Multi-joint contact dynamics, actuator models, and high-performance simulation using **MuJoCo**.
- **Physical AI & Embodied Systems**: Sim-to-real transfer, reinforcement learning for robotic control, and spatial intelligence.
- **Core Engineering**: Scalable distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, and developer tooling at Netlify.
